# venue — the target workshop

Invented example content.

**Format:** 6 pages including references, two-column, the workshop's own class file.
The limit is hard: over-length submissions are desk-rejected without review.

**References:** no explicit limit, but they count against the 6 pages.

**Review regime:** single-anonymous. Author names appear on the submitted PDF; reviewer
names do not. A repository URL is therefore allowed — but see the artifact decision below.

**Deadline:** abstract one week before the full paper. The abstract is binding: the title
and author list cannot change afterwards.

**AI disclosure:** required, as a sentence in the acknowledgements naming what assistance
was used. No AI system may be listed as an author.
